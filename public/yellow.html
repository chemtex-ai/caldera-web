Tasting Notes

If you enjoy a softer beer with a citrus lift, start here.

Bright citrus, gentle spice and soft wheat come together in an easygoing Belgian-style wheat beer.

Aroma. Fresh orange peel and coriander over soft clove and cereal wheat.

Mouthfeel. A long-lasting white head and a medium, creamy body mellow the hop bitterness, with smooth carbonation and a clean, refreshing finish.
