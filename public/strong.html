Tasting Notes

Choose this if you prefer a fuller, malt-led beer.

A fuller-bodied lager with rich malt character and a smooth finish, for those who prefer a bolder beer.

Aroma. Toasted malt and a soft roast with a hint of caramel warmth.

Mouthfeel. A fuller body with smooth carbonation and a warming, rounded finish — bold but balanced.
